"Legends in Lederhosen" Chris Evans, BBC Radio 2

The ultimate five-piece Oompah band - we are Oompah Brass!

We are the UK's original rock 'n' roll Oompah band, a 5-piece brass ensemble specialising in virtuosic, high-energy versions of rock and pop classics like you’ve never heard before!

Since forming in 2006, we've been transforming the traditional Oompah sound, blending it with legends of rock and pop such as Queen, The Killers, Britney Spears, The Beatles, Taylor Swift, and many more. Renowned for our electrifying performances and high energy, we've been captivating audiences and redefining what it means to be an Oompah band. We were also proud winners of the prestigious International Haizetara Street Band competition

'Genuinely my favourite band!' Alex Horne, Taskmaster

Dubbed 'Legends in Lederhosen' by none other than Chris Evans on BBC Radio 2, we have travelled the world, playing over 100 gigs a year, and thrilling audiences from intimate Bierkellers to large, international festivals. Our unique Bavarian spin on classics like Bohemian Rhapsody and Stairway to Heaven has earned us a reputation for being more than just a band - we're an experience!

Check out our live dates, join us for a night of unforgettable music and laughter, and experience the unrivalled Oompah show!
